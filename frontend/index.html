PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE

From inbox overload to intelligent action.

A three-step machine learning pipeline analyzes semantics, predicts urgency with statistical models, and extracts explicit action items with LLMs.

STEP 01

Analyze

Extract useful signals from email content and context. Tokenizes message text, isolates headers, removes noise, and formats semantic vectors.

TF-IDF N-Gram Vectorizer • Semantic Text Normalization
STEP 02

Prioritize

Use machine learning to classify messages by importance. Logistic regression outputs calibrated confidence scores to separate urgent tasks from routine mail.

Scikit-Learn Classifier • Calibrated Confidence Bounds
STEP 03

Take Action

Surface the messages that deserve attention first. Gemini extracts explicit deadlines and structured to-dos, triggering Telegram alerts for anything due within 24 hours.

Gemini Extraction • Telegram Instant Push Alerts
