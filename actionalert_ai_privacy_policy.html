Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

ActionAlert AI helps users organize and prioritize email using AI-powered features. This policy explains what information the app may access and how it is used.

1. Information we access

Depending on the features you enable, ActionAlert AI may process:

  • Gmail information authorized by you, such as message metadata and email content needed to fetch, summarize, or prioritize messages.
  • Information you provide directly when using the app.
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Data required to deliver notifications through Telegram, if you configure that feature.

2. How we use information

We use information to fetch and display emails, identify messages that may need attention, generate summaries or classifications, provide configured notifications, maintain the service, and diagnose errors.

3. Gmail and Google user data

ActionAlert AI accesses Google user data only after you authorize the requested permissions. We use that data to provide the app's email-related features described above. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

We do not permit humans to read your email content unless you explicitly provide it for support, it is necessary for security purposes, or applicable law requires it. Where an AI service provider processes email content to generate a requested feature, the content is sent only as needed to provide that feature.

ActionAlert AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that operate features you use, such as hosting, database, AI processing, and notification delivery providers. These providers receive information only as needed to provide the relevant service. We do not sell personal information.

5. Storage and security

Information may be stored in the app's configured database and hosting environment. We use reasonable safeguards to protect data and credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and users should not share passwords, API keys, or access tokens.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with legal obligations. You may stop access by revoking the app's Google permissions in your Google Account. To request deletion of app-held data, contact us using the email below. The app operator should configure and follow a deletion process for stored data and tokens.

7. Your choices

  • You can revoke Google account access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings.
  • You can stop using the app and disable configured Telegram notifications.
  • You can contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information associated with your use of the app.

8. Children's privacy

ActionAlert AI is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
